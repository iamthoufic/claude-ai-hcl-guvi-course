# Claude for Real Work Tasks

How we can use Claude to create documents, PDFs, Excel sheets, and other office-related work materials. Many people use Claude for both personal and professional use cases.

## 1. Drafting Emails and Messages

When writing a professional email with Claude, this is how you should draft the prompt:

```text
Write a professional email to my team informing them that tomorrow's client meeting has moved from 2 PM to 4 PM.
Keep the tone polite and concise.
```

## 2. Summarizing Documents

**Weak prompt:**

```text
Summarize this.
```

**How to get a good summary:**

```text
Summarize this meeting transcript into key decisions, action items, deadlines, and unresolved issues.
[Upload the meeting transcript]
```

Claude will give you all the relevant information you asked for.

You can also upload a research article and ask Claude to summarize it.

## 3. Brainstorming

You can also run brainstorming sessions with Claude:

```text
Generate 10 SaaS business ideas related to AI productivity tools for students.
```

To get constructive ideas instead of simple agreement, ask Claude to challenge your thinking — for example, "Point out the weaknesses and risks of each idea."

## 4. Editing and Improving Content

Follow-up prompt:

```text
Improve the clarity and readability of the content you have generated.
```

> **Tip:** Define the tone and the audience you want Claude to write the message for.

## Summary

- This module focuses on using Claude for practical workplace tasks such as writing emails, editing reports, and summarizing content.
- Learners are taught how to use Claude for brainstorming so that it provides constructive ideas and challenges instead of simply agreeing.
- The module demonstrates research and synthesis workflows, including summarizing long documents and comparing different options efficiently.
- The project task asks learners to test their system prompt on three real work-related activities: drafting an email, summarizing notes, and generating ideas for a current challenge.
