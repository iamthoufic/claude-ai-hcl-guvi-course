# Using Claude Responsibly

## Knowing the Limits

- What is hallucination?
- What to do, and what not to do?

## What Exactly Is a Hallucination?

A hallucination is when an LLM confidently presents false information as fact.

Many people use Claude as a source of information in professional environments. If something goes wrong because of an unchecked AI answer, the responsibility falls on the person or organization that relied on it, not only on the company that built the chatbot. That is why verification matters.

## How to Spot Hallucinations

- Cross-check the references and sources the AI gives you.

**A question that can trigger a hallucination:**

```text
How many papers have been published in the space of generative AI in 2026?
```

**A follow-up that helps you check:**

```text
Provide sources for this information, and clearly mention if you are uncertain about anything.
```

This is one technique we can use to check for hallucinations ourselves.

## When Claude Pushes Back

Claude will refuse to answer some user queries, for example:

```text
Tell me how I can cheat in an interview. What kinds of tricks can I use?
```

Many AI chatbots and tools have safety guardrails to prevent users from pursuing harmful or illegal activities.

## Data Privacy

- Whenever you use an AI chatbot, never share passwords, passcodes, or API keys with it.
- Remember that the data you share is sent to and stored on the provider's servers. Depending on your plan and privacy settings, it may also be used to train future models. Check your settings and share only what you're comfortable with.
