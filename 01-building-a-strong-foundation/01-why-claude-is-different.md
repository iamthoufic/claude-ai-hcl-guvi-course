# Why Is Claude Different?

## Introduction

- Claude is one of the most powerful AI tools currently available.
- It is developed by a company called Anthropic.
- Most people today are familiar with AI tools and chatbots such as ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot.
- Claude has built its own identity in the AI community because of its thinking capabilities, its long-context capacity, and the quality of the output it produces compared to other AI tools.

## Key Questions

- Why is Claude different?
- What are the core differences between Claude and other AI tools such as Gemini and ChatGPT?

## What Is Claude?

- Claude is an advanced AI assistant developed by Anthropic.
- It is designed for natural conversations, reasoning, and productive tasks.
- It is widely used for writing, coding, research, and AI-assisted workflows.
- It is known for strong context understanding and safer AI responses.
- It is popular among developers, researchers, and content creators.

Claude is known for producing human-oriented, well-aligned responses to queries in a structured manner.

### Common Use Cases

- Coding
- Creating content
- Deep research

## Claude Models

- **Claude Haiku** → The fastest model, optimized for speed and lightweight tasks.
- **Claude Sonnet** → A balanced model for coding, reasoning, and daily workflows.
- **Claude Opus** → The most powerful of the three, built for deep reasoning and complex tasks.
- Different models are optimized for speed, intelligence, and cost efficiency.
- Users choose a model depending on their workload and performance requirements.

Mythos and Fable are other notable, powerful models from Anthropic.

## Claude vs. Other AI Tools

- Strong long-context understanding for large conversations and documents.
- Natural, human-like responses with better readability.
- Excellent at summarizing reports, PDFs, and research material.
- Produces structured reasoning and step-by-step explanations.
- Highly useful for coding, brainstorming, and workflow automation.

## How Claude Reads Your Prompt

- It understands the main intent behind your request.
- It detects your tone and adapts its communication style accordingly.
- It uses previous conversation context to maintain continuity.
- It prioritizes key instructions and important requirements.
- It learns patterns from the examples and formatting you provide.

These are the main building blocks of a better prompt.

## Common Beginner Mistakes

- Writing very short prompts with insufficient detail.
- Providing little context about the actual requirement.
- Combining too many instructions into a single prompt.
- Not refining or iterating on AI-generated responses.
- Treating AI like a search engine instead of a collaborator.

> **Don't be lazy when you prompt Claude.**
